Farm Produce

What We Produce

We supply fresh, organic, and biosecure farm produce to local retailers, restaurants, and individuals across Wakiso and Kampala.

Poultry Division

Rearing layers and broilers in highly hygienic, automated environments. We supply fresh, graded eggs and premium broiler meat daily.

  • High-yield layer strains
  • Clean, automated feeding loops
  • Graded, double-inspected egg trays
Order Eggs & Chicken

Piggery Division

Specialized breeding of high-grade piglets and quality pork. Reared using biosecure pens, clean feeds, and sustainable waste-to-energy management.

  • Healthy, active weaner piglets
  • Cross-breed vigor (Large White / Landrace)
  • Biogas waste recycle loops
Order Piglets

Crop Husbandry

Cultivating premium Ugandan Matooke (green bananas) and yellow field maize. Grown utilizing organic manure compost sourced directly from our farm divisions.

  • Massive, heavy Matooke bunches
  • Sweet, nutrient-rich yellow maize
  • 100% organic waste fertilization

Training Center

Fostering Modern Commercial Farming

We operate as a practical instruction node for active and entry-level farmers in Wakiso. From biosecure disease barriers to renewable farm fuels, our methods maximize yields while conserving local natural resources.

Hands-on livestock husbandry lessons
Financial management for micro-farms
Waste recycling infrastructure design
Join Next Class

Strict Biosecurity & Hygiene

Implementing clean-entry locks, footbaths, vaccine tracks, and sanitization grids to keep bird flock and pig breeds healthy.

Eco-Friendly Biogas Heating

Collecting pig slurry to fuel gas digesters that power warmth-brooders for baby chicks, reducing charcoal dependency.

PoultryTechUganda Affiliate

Partnering with Dutch and Ugandan entities to implement high-yield technologies and elevate poultry sector standards.
