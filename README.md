# Biwooma Farmers Limited - Official Website

Official website for **Biwooma Farmers Limited**, a commercial farm and agricultural training center located in Namaina, Gayaza, Wakiso District, Uganda.

## 🌿 Overview

Biwooma Farmers Limited specializes in:
- **Poultry Division:** High-yield layers, broilers, graded eggs, and biosecure brooding environments.
- **Piggery Division:** Specialized breeding of high-grade piglets (Large White / Landrace) and IMO (Indigenous Micro-Organism) deep-bedding methods.
- **Crop Husbandry:** Premium Ugandan Matooke (green bananas) and yellow maize nourished by organic manure.
- **Renewable Energy & Biogas:** Waste-to-energy bio-digester loops converting slurry into clean gas for cooking and brooding.
- **Agritourism & Training Center:** Practical 2-hour guided farm visits and agricultural education for commercial and aspiring farmers.

## 🚀 Key Website Features

- **Interactive WhatsApp Order Estimator:** Visitors can calculate custom quantities for Matooke, Piglets, Live Chicken, and Egg trays, and dispatch formatted inquiries straight to WhatsApp.
- **Agritourism Booking:** Direct reservation calculator for guided 2-hour farm visits with instant WhatsApp dispatch.
- **Multimedia Farm Activity Gallery:** Filterable media gallery (All Media, Farm Videos, Photos) with interactive pop-up video player showcasing broiler preparations, IMO pig rearing, and flock feeding.
- **Responsive & Modern Design:** Mobile-friendly, high-performance layout built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern JavaScript.
- **Direct Form Submission:** Inquiry contact section with live status dispatch.
